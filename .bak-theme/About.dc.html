ABOUT STARLIGHT · WHY WE EXIST

The future of broking isn't less human.
It's less manual.

Commercial insurance runs on expertise.
We're giving expertise its time back.

Brokers do far more than sell insurance. They collect documents, untangle complex risk information, chase missing details, assemble submissions, negotiate with underwriters, compare quotes, manage renewals, issue certificates, and answer a thousand service requests across the life of every policy.

Too much of that work still lives in inboxes, spreadsheets, PDFs, shared drives, and legacy systems. The problem was never the insurance workflow. The problem is how much manual work is trapped inside it.

That's why we're building Starlight.

THE SYSTEM

Documents become data. Data becomes completed work.

Commercial insurance doesn't begin with a clean form. It begins with PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, photographs, loss runs, prior policies, and conversations — important details repeated, inconsistent, or buried across dozens of pages. Starlight is a modern broker management system designed around that reality.

It brings the broker, the client, the risk, the documents, and the work around a policy into one connected system — then uses what it understands to move the file forward.

01 · RECEIVED
The raw file

Clients share documents through a guided intake — or Starlight's agents call, text, and chase what's missing.

02 · UNDERSTOOD
Structured risk data

Facts are extracted across every document type, cross-checked, connected — and the gaps are flagged before they cost you a market.

03 · COMPLETED
Submit-ready work

Applications filled, submission packages assembled, certificates issued, client documents generated — in seconds, not afternoons.

04 · CARRIED FORWARD
Never repeated

Everything learned flows into quoting, servicing, renewals, and claims. The account gets smarter every year it stays with you.

The goal is not to extract data.
The goal is to complete work.

OUR APPROACH TO AI

AI that works inside the workflow — not beside it.

AI in insurance should do more than answer questions or summarize documents. It should understand the work being performed and carry it toward completion — through the channels, forms, and processes the industry already runs on.

Behind the work,
never in the way

Brokers shouldn't have to become prompt engineers, redesign their agency around a tool, or abandon workflows that already make sense. The technology stays behind the work.

Context,
not chat

It recognizes information across ACORDs, loss runs, schedules, and emails; connects related facts; and identifies what's missing — because it knows what the next step requires.

Completion,
not summaries

The output isn't a paragraph about your file. It's the filled application, the assembled packet, the issued certificate, the sent follow-up — work, finished.

THE FUTURE WE'RE BUILDING

By 2030, the file will work itself.
The broker will work the relationship.

McKinsey projects that by 2030, underwriting for most standard commercial risks will be near-instant — quotes assembled in minutes, not weeks. We're building the agency side of that future: a broker receives a complex new opportunity, and Starlight carries the operational work from first client interaction through submission, placement, servicing, and renewal.

→ The client provides information once.
→ Documents become data. Data becomes completed work.
→ Work carries forward instead of being repeated.

Our ambition is not to replace brokers. The broker stays at the center of the decisions, the advice, and the relationships. Our ambition is to give brokers a system capable of doing the work that should never have required so much of their time in the first place.
