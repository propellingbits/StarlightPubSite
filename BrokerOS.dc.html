WHY IT'S BUILT THIS WAY

Not a bolt-on. A system of record.

Most "AI for insurance" tools are a thin layer calling someone else's BMS or carrier API. When that vendor changes an endpoint, a field, or a policy, the integration breaks — silently, mid-conversation, with no obligation to fix it for you.

Starlight owns the data model end to end. Your AI agents work your data, not someone else's API — so they don't break when a carrier updates their system, and they don't need rebuilding every time a broker migrates in.
