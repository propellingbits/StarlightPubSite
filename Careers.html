HOW WE WORK · THE UNGLAMOROUS TRUTH

What you would be signing up for.

THE GOOD PART
You talk to customers. Everyone here does, engineers included. Decisions take hours, not quarters. There is no committee. Your work ships. Usually the same week you write it. Real equity. No outside investors means no down-round arithmetic.
THE PART OTHERS LEAVE OUT
Salaries are competitive, not FAANG. Equity is where the upside lives. No support layer to hide behind. If it breaks, you fix it. Insurance is genuinely intricate. Expect two months of feeling slow. A bootstrapped company can fail. We would rather you knew that now.
